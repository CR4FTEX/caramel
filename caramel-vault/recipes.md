# Recipes

How Caramel replaces, adds and removes recipes, and the traps each one hides. The code is `CaramelRecipeProvider` in `src/client/java/io/github/cr4ftex/caramel/client/datagen/`; what it generates, and why, is ideas C-001 (stairs), C-002 (slabs), C-003 (pressure plates and carpets), C-004 (fences), C-005 (fence gates), C-006 (doors and trapdoors), C-007 (buttons) and C-008 (walls) in `docs/DESIGN.md`. Everything below was checked in Minecraft 26.3 with Fabric API 0.161, in bytecode and by loading the real data packs in a scratch copy.

## How recipes load

- Recipes and advancements are reloadable registries, loaded in the same batch by `RegistryDataLoader` (`RELOADABLE_REGISTRIES`). `RecipeManager` only wraps the frozen `minecraft:recipe` registry; it reads no files itself.
- A recipe is `data/<namespace>/recipe/<path>.json`, with id `<namespace>:<path>`.
- Fabric registers Caramel as a required, hidden data pack. `ModPackResourcesUtil.refreshAutoEnabledPacks` re-inserts every hidden mod pack right after `vanilla`, in pack-id order, in new worlds, existing worlds and safe mode alike, so Caramel always sits above `vanilla`. For each id only the top pack's file is read (`listMatchingResources`), so Caramel's `data/minecraft/recipe/<id>.json` replaces vanilla's.
- Removing a recipe: the file at the vanilla id carries `"fabric:load_conditions":[{"condition":"fabric:false"}]` in front of the vanilla recipe body (for example `src/main/generated/data/minecraft/recipe/andesite_stairs_from_andesite_stonecutting.json`). Fabric drops the entry before parsing (`RegistryLoadTaskPendingRegistrationMixin`), and vanilla's lower file is never read. `FabricRecipeProvider.withConditions(output, ResourceConditions.alwaysFalse())` writes it, on the recipe and on its advancement.
- Only an advancement's `recipe_unlocked` criterion binds its recipe as a registry entry: `RecipeUnlockedTrigger$TriggerInstance` decodes `recipes` with `Recipe.LIST_CODEC`, a registry holder set. `AdvancementRewards` decodes its `recipes` with `Recipe.KEY_CODEC`, a plain key, and `ServerPlayer.awardRecipesByKey` skips a key with no recipe. A missing recipe named only in rewards loads without a log line (scratch copy).
- A removed or replaced stonecutter recipe needs no menu or client change. The stonecutter's list is `RecipeManager.stonecutterRecipes`, rebuilt from the recipe registry by `finalizeRecipeLoading` and sent to clients in `ClientboundUpdateRecipesPacket`. Minecraft 26.3 has no stonecutter `RecipePropertySet` (`javap`).

## Datagen

- `FabricRecipeProvider.getRecipeIdentifier` rewrites every recipe id to the mod's namespace by default, `minecraft:oak_stairs` included. Caramel overrides it to return the id unchanged, so `minecraft:` ids can replace vanilla recipes.
- The shaped, shapeless, stonecutting (`SingleItemRecipeBuilder`), cooking, smithing, transmute and custom-crafting builders save an unlock advancement, `<namespace>:recipes/<category folder>/<path>`. Without an `unlockedBy` they throw "No way of obtaining recipe <id>" (`RecipeUnlockAdvancementBuilder.build`). `SpecialRecipeBuilder` saves one only after `unlockedBy`, and `BrewingRecipeBuilder` saves none.
- `save(output, String)` throws when the string equals the default id; `save(output, ResourceKey)` does not.
- Saving two recipes under one id in the same `FabricRecipeProvider` stops datagen with `IllegalStateException` "Duplicate registration for <key>" (`FabricRecipeProvider$FabricBootstrapContext.register`, fabric-data-generation-api-v1 27.2.4).
- Shaped recipes match their left-right mirror image and nothing else (`ShapedRecipePattern.matches`, `Util.isSymmetrical`). A 2x2 pattern matches anywhere in the 3x3 grid, and in the inventory's 2x2 grid.
- Empty rows and columns around a pattern do not count: vanilla's waxed chiseled coppers, written `" M "`/`" M "`, match 2 stacked slabs in a 1-wide grid, as `"#"`/`"#"` does (`recipe.matches` in `SlabsRecipeGameTest.removedRecipes` finds them). Search vanilla's recipes for a shape by matching, never by comparing pattern strings.
- `BlockFamilies.getAllFamilies()` holds each family's variants. In 26.3, 99 families have a slab, 14 a fence (12 wooden families and nether bricks as `FENCE`, bamboo as `CUSTOM_FENCE`), the 13 wooden families a door and a trapdoor, 15 a button (the 13 wooden families, stone and polished blackstone), and 32 a wall. The iron and copper doors and trapdoors are in no family (`javap -c` on `BlockFamilies`). Vanilla crafts the smooth stone slab outside any family. It also hand-writes some recipes the family variants suggest, so the variants alone miss them:
  - The chiseled variant is crafted from the family's slab (`BlockFamily.Variant.getBaseVariantForCrafting`, read through `getBaseBlockForCrafting`).
  - The pillar and mosaic variants are crafted from the base block. Vanilla still makes the purpur pillar and bamboo mosaic from 2 slabs, and the quartz pillar from 2 quartz blocks, by explicit calls in `VanillaRecipeProvider`.
  - The waxed chiseled coppers are in no family (`waxedChiseled`).
  - Families built with `dontGenerateCraftingRecipe()` (stone bricks, sandstone, red sandstone, quartz, purpur) get their recipes by hand, and their unlock criteria vary recipe by recipe (vanilla's `advancement/recipes/`). The stairs and slabs of stone bricks unlock on the `#minecraft:stone_bricks` tag (`has_stone_bricks`), and those of sandstone, red sandstone and quartz on one criterion per accepted block, none of which the family walk writes. The chiseled stone bricks unlock on `has_tag`, and the quartz pillar, chiseled quartz block and chiseled red sandstone on three criteria each. Their walls, cut sandstones and quartz bricks unlock on the walk's own `has_<base>`, as purpur's recipes do on `has_purpur_block`.

  Found by logging every family's variants from a datagen run, and by `javap -c` on `VanillaRecipeProvider`.
- Vanilla's stonecutter recipes for a family's variants come from `generateStonecutterRecipe(family, variant, base)` over the families with `shouldGenerateStonecutterRecipe`. For a `POLISHED`, `CUT`, `BRICKS`, `TILES`, `PILLAR` or `COBBLED` variant it also walks that variant's own family with the same base, so the walk reaches the recipes that cut from another family's block, such as `stone_brick_wall_from_stone_stonecutting` (`javap -c` on `RecipeProvider`). `VanillaRecipeProvider` writes the rest with direct calls, such as `polished_basalt_from_basalt_stonecutting` and a `WeatheringCopperCollection.zipApply` over the 8 cut copper stairs cut from a Block of Copper (`javap -c` on `VanillaRecipeProvider`). Every one ends in `stonecutterResultFromBase`, which `CaramelRecipeProvider` overrides to write only stairs (removed) and walls (2 per block). Its own walk and copper call reach all 103 stonecutter recipes that make stairs (95 and 8), and the walk all 57 that make walls; datagen stops if either count changes.
- Vanilla waxes each copper block or item with its own shapeless recipe, `<waxed id>_from_honeycomb`: the unwaxed one and 1 honeycomb make 1 waxed one, 60 recipes in all (`data/minecraft/recipe/*_from_honeycomb.json`), separate from the recipe that crafts the unwaxed one. `DoorsAndTrapdoorsGameTest.waxingStaysOneForOne` checks that the copper door and trapdoor ones load as vanilla's.
- `RecipeProvider`'s helpers, such as `getCraftingCriterionName`, `getBaseBlockForCrafting`, `pressurePlateBuilder` and `generateCraftingRecipe`, are private or protected in Mojang's jar and public in the jar Caramel compiles against, `.gradle/loom-cache/minecraftMaven/.../minecraft-common-*.jar`, through Fabric API's transitive access wideners (`javap -p` on both). Read the API from the loom-cache jar.
- Vanilla's builders rebuild vanilla's recipe bodies for a removal, such as `chiseledBuilder` with the family's group and `getCraftingCriterionName`. Fabric drops a removed file before reading its body, so a removed advancement's criteria need not match vanilla's: C-002 removes four with simpler criteria (`docs/DESIGN.md`, C-002).
- A recipe that replaces a vanilla one keeps vanilla's unlock advancement byte for byte when it is saved with vanilla's criterion (`getCraftingCriterionName`, or `getHasName` of the vanilla ingredient). Its recipe book entry then unlocks on vanilla's ingredient, not the new one: C-003's oak pressure plate takes oak slabs and unlocks on `has_planks`.
- The recipe book shows one button per recipe book category and non-empty group string; a recipe with an empty group gets a button of its own (`RecipeManager.unpackRecipeInfo`, `ClientRecipeBook.categorizeAndGroupRecipes`).

## Traps

- **A removed recipe whose unlock advancement stays stops every world from loading.** The advancement's `recipe_unlocked` criterion names the recipe as a registry entry, so the registry freeze fails with "Unbound values in registry ResourceKey[minecraft:root / minecraft:recipe]: [...]" and "Failed to load registries due to errors". Safe mode does not help, because the Caramel pack is required. Deleting a generated recipe file without its advancement fails the same way. Remove both, always.
- **With the identity `getRecipeIdentifier`, a save without an explicit key silently replaces a vanilla recipe.** Datagen and loading raise no error: the recipe lands on the result item's `minecraft:` id and replaces the vanilla recipe there. Every new recipe passes `ResourceKey.create(Registries.RECIPE, Caramel.id(...))`. The game test `VanillaOverridesGameTest.onlyExpectedVanillaRecipesAreReplacedOrRemoved` fails if Caramel's pack holds a recipe or advancement file at any other `minecraft:` id, and names each one: "Caramel's data pack has recipe minecraft:acacia_planks, which <ideas> do not touch", where <ideas> is `VanillaOverridesGameTest.IDEAS` (see [[gametest]]).
- **Without the identity override, datagen and loading raise no error either.** Every id moves to `caramel:`, vanilla's recipes stay active beside the new ones, and `data/minecraft/recipe` stays empty. The game tests `onlyExpectedVanillaRecipesAreReplacedOrRemoved`, `oldSixBlockStaircaseGivesNothing` and `noStonecuttingRecipeMakesStairs` fail on it (scratch copy).
- **An unknown condition id counts as passing.** Fabric logs an ERROR "Failed to parse resource conditions ... skipping" (`ResourceConditionsImpl.applyResourceConditions`) and parses the body. A file that carries the vanilla body, as `withConditions` writes it, loads the recipe again, and `noStonecuttingRecipeMakesStairs` fails with "1 of 249 stonecutter recipes failed" (scratch copy). A file that holds only the condition fails with "No key type", and the whole load fails. Build conditions with `ResourceConditions` methods such as `alwaysFalse()`, never a hand-typed id.
- **Two recipes that match the same grid both load, and nothing reports the overlap.** The crafting table calls `RecipeManager.getRecipeFor` with the recipe it used last (`CraftingMenu.slotChangedCraftingGrid`): that recipe wins while it still matches, and otherwise the first match wins. The game tests fail any grid they craft that matches more than one recipe (`RecipeTestSupport.craft` and `Crafted.gives`).
- **Removing a recipe silently strands the recipes that use its result.** Datagen, loading and the game tests raise nothing: when C-002 removed the only recipe that makes bamboo mosaic, `minecraft:bamboo_mosaic_slab` and `minecraft:bamboo_mosaic_stairs` went on loading with no error. Before removing a recipe, search every vanilla recipe's `key` and `ingredients` for its result (`docs/DESIGN.md`, C-002, "What can no longer be made"). `PressurePlatesAndCarpetsGameTest.detectorRailCraftsAgain` checks the one such recipe C-003 brought back.
- **A recipe that loses its unlock advancement still crafts, silently.** The recipe book never shows it. `UnlockAdvancementsGameTest` fails when a `caramel:` recipe has no advancement, or more than one, at `caramel:recipes/<folder>/<path>`: "Recipe caramel:oak_planks_from_oak_slab has 0 unlock advancements [], expected 1" (deleted file, reproduced).
- **A walk over one variant silently misses the family that holds its custom twin.** Bamboo's fence, fence gate and hanging signs are its family's `CUSTOM_FENCE`, `CUSTOM_FENCE_GATE`, `CUSTOM_HANGING_SIGN` and `CUSTOM_WALL_HANGING_SIGN` variants, the only custom variants a 26.3 family uses (`BlockFamily$Builder.customFence` and its siblings, `javap -c` on `BlockFamilies`). A walk filtering on `FENCE` finds 13 of the 14 fence recipes and leaves vanilla's bamboo fence recipe loaded, with no error. `CaramelRecipeProvider` counts what each walk writes and stops datagen on a mismatch: a `FENCE`-only walk stops with "Expected 14 block families with fences, found 13". Check a family walk's count against the vanilla recipes it should replace.
- **In 26.3, a copper constant in `Blocks` or `Items` is a collection of eight, not a block or an item.** 15 fields of `Blocks`, among them `COPPER_BLOCK`, `COPPER_DOOR` and `COPPER_TRAPDOOR`, and the same 15 of `Items` are `WeatheringCopperCollection`s, and neither class has a `WAXED_`, `EXPOSED_`, `WEATHERED_` or `OXIDIZED_` field (`javap` on the loom-cache jar). Used as a block, one fails to compile with "incompatible types: bad type in conditional expression" inside `?:`, and "Object cannot be converted to ItemLike" after a `Map.of` that mixes it with blocks. Pick one with `weathering()` or `waxed()`, then `unaffected()`, `exposed()`, `weathered()` or `oxidized()`: the plain Block of Copper is `Blocks.COPPER_BLOCK.weathering().unaffected()` (`CaramelRecipeProvider.doorsAndTrapdoors`).
- **An existing world logs one ERROR per removed recipe a player had learned**, at that player's login: "Tried to load unrecognized recipe: <id> removed now." `ServerRecipeBook.loadRecipes` logs each saved key that `RecipeManager.byKey` no longer finds, drops it and goes on (read in bytecode, `javap`).
